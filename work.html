VeoVJ

Experiment, AI, Global Campaign 2025

I conceived and coded VeoVJ, a tool for live-remixing visuals generated with Veo, Google's gen video model. By setting the model's early, often surreal 6-second outputs to music, I transformed them into cohesive, high-energy audiovisual performances.

This project went from a creative spark to a mainstage reality, opening for major events including the Google Cloud Next Gen Keynote, the I/O Developer Keynote, and Google Marketing Live across 7 performances and three continents. The tool has powered experiences seen by over 6 million people online, and I've performed with it live for audiences of over 30,000.

Logo by the brilliant Jocelyn Zhao and motion graphics by the talented Tiff Tedy.

"What's Your Vibe?" — Mainstage I/O Talk

Talk, AI 2025

For Google I/O's mainstage, I co-created and performed a completely live demo of the new Gemini Canvas features. Alongside my colleague Aaron Wade, we designed "What's Your Vibe?", a high-energy game show where we were challenged to build an app with randomly selected vibes.

In minutes, we built a functional, poetic, vintage-style camera app and shared it instantly with the audience by having Canvas generate its own QR code on the fly.

The randomizer was designed by the wonderful Tania Lili.

30 Days of Gemini Canvas

Campaign, AI 2025

In partnership with Google Developer Relations, I pioneered the #30DaysOfGeminiCanvas challenge to showcase the creative power and speed of the new API.

Every day for a month, I would "live vibe-code" a new generative toy or tool, demonstrating how quickly ideas can be brought to life. The daily challenge was designed to demystify generative AI development and inspire a community of builders to experiment.
