about:

Hi, I'm a Tina Tarighian.

My day job is making demos: what can this technology do? My art practice is making spectacles: what can this technology say? The two feed each other indefinitely: the system itself becomes my medium.

My work takes formats already metabolized by culture and redirects their momentum. A purity test that measures not sexual promiscuity but depth of entanglement with AI. An email to your ex with a 0.0001% chance of sending. A dating app that only lets you match via instagram algorithms, the surveilled and taxonomized residue of your data recycled for love. Each work borrows the compulsion of an existing format and aims it somewhere more honest.

My practice spans software, physical space, and the internet as a distribution system in itself. It has appeared in galleries, on television, at music festivals, in inboxes. These are different surfaces for the same questions, and the questions travel. Millions took a quiz to determine their standing on a technology nobody fully understands yet. A few hundred signups were looking to find their scrollmates. The work goes as far as it needs to.

The formats I work in already have your attention. They already have your data. They already know what you do at midnight and who you looked up twice. I am just making sure you notice.

formal bio

Tina Tarighian is an artist whose practice moves between demo and spectacle, repurposing the formats of contemporary technology to examine what they do and what they reveal. Working in software, installation, and internet infrastructure, she builds inside the systems she examines, producing work that functions at scale and has reached millions. Her work has been exhibited at TIAT Place, San Francisco; Grace Exhibition Space, New York; and in Scan Artist (2024), a solo performance in New York. She developed custom video collage software that debuted at Coachella, Governors Ball, Portola Festival, and SXSW, and she held a performance residency at Pianos NY, New York. She has been invited to speak on her work at the New Museum’s DEMO, The Do Lectures Wales, and ARTECHOUSE, among others. She is a member of New INC Year 11 Cohort, a 2026 EY Emergent Futures Fellow, and the recipient of the inaugural 2025 INTL International Creative Award in the web category.

She is a Senior Creative Technologist at Google Creative Lab and is based in New York.
